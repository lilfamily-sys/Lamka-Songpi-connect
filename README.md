Welcome to LAMKA CONNECT!

Our goal is to connect people across Churachandpur (Lamka) with useful local information and services.

Our Services

- Healthcare and emergency information
- Government services
- Banks and post offices
- Education and scholarships
- Transport and utilities
- Local businesses and shops
- Jobs and community information

Languages

- English
- Thadou
- Paite
- Hmar

Our Mission

To make reliable local information accessible to everyone and support a better future for our community.

Note: LAMKA CONNECT is an independent community information project, not an official government website. Please verify important details before visiting or contacting a service.
